everything comes at us at once.
how do we make sense of any of it?
we save it to make sense of later.
later never comes.
so we live on headlines.
and hot takes that thrive on outrage.
The Lede slows us down.
it reads all of it.
it unburies the lede.
so we know if it's worth our attention.
sometimes it isn't.
when it is, we dig deeper.
it looks in the research and the reporting.
and lists every source.
hear the author out.
then see what the sources say.
and decide for ourselves.
